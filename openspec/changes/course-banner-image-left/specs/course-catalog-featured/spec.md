## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Featured banner shows the cover on the left
The catalog featured banner slide SHALL render the course cover image on the LEFT and the merchandising copy (title, pitch, CTA) on the RIGHT on desktop, matching the legacy FTES banner. On mobile it stacks to a column with the cover hidden.

#### Scenario: Desktop banner layout
- **WHEN** a featured banner slide renders on a desktop-width viewport
- **THEN** the cover image is on the left and the title/pitch/CTA are on the right
